Sable Line — a round-based tactical FPS in three.js. Plant the spike at Site A or Site B, or wipe the enemy team; earn money each round and buy rifles, SMGs and grenades between rounds. Five defender bots hold angles, rotate on the plant and try to defuse, while two ally bots push with you across a dusk foundry with two bomb sites, a mid lane and real sightlines. Headshots hurt, firing on the move punishes you, and stopping to aim pays.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Click to lock the mouse. WASD to move, Shift to walk silently, Ctrl/C to crouch, Space to jump, click to fire, R to reload, right-click to aim, B to open the buy menu, G to throw a grenade, E to plant or defuse the spike. First team to win the round takes the point — most rounds wins the match.
